                    ℹ️ Chat List Refresh Modes
                
      
                
            
      

            
      
                
                
      
                    
      
                        ⚡ Quick Refresh (~1s)
                    
      
                    
      
                        Queries Telegram servers for recent dialogs, exempting all already-cached chats from heavy
                        message-counting queries. Discovers newly created chats, newly joined channels/groups, and title
                        changes in ~1 second.
                    
      
                    
      
                        💡 Best used: daily or routine checks to quickly detect newly added chats or renamed channels.
                    
      
                
      

                
                
      
                    
      
                        🔄 Full Telegram Refresh (15–30s)
                    
      
                    
      
                        Performs a deep scan of all dialogs across your entire Telegram account history from scratch.
                        Re-checks exact server message counts for every chat, supergroup migrations, and wallpapers.
                    
      
                    
      
                        🛡️ Non-destructive: never alters or deletes your archived messages, database records, or
                        downloaded media.
                    
      
                
      

                
                
      
                    
      
                        ⚠️ Important Caveats & Edge Cases
                    
      
                    
        
                        
      • Quick Refresh inspects recent dialogs. If you
                            joined an older, completely inactive chat with no recent messages, it might be further down
                            in history and require a Full Refresh.
        • 
                        
      • First time run: If no local cache exists yet,
                            Quick Refresh automatically falls back to an initial full scan.
        • 
                        
      • Missing a very old or inactive chat? Run a
                            Full Refresh to scan your complete account history.
        • 
                    
      

                    
      
                        Need a deep scan of your entire account history?
